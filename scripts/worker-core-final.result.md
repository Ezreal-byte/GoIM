**High Findings**
- **[Confirmed] Former owner can roll back group ownership by truncating the proof chain** — `internal/core/groups.go:456`, `internal/core/groups.go:42`. After an A→B transfer, A can send a higher-epoch `group_state` with `OwnerID=A`, `OwnerKey=root`, and `Ownership=[]`, signed by the retained root key. `verifyGroup` accepts the self-consistent truncated chain, and line 456 installs it without requiring `g.Ownership` to extend `old.Ownership` or identifying the sender against the persisted owner. **Remedy:** require an exact prefix-extension of `old.Ownership`, derive the authorized signer from that persisted chain, and apply the check to every group-install path, including invite/offer.

- **[Confirmed; timing-gated] Ownership target can leave during handoff and become an unusable owner** — `internal/core/groups.go:278`, `internal/core/groups.go:447`, `internal/core/groups.go:74`. After B accepts ownership but before A’s proof arrives, `LeaveGroup` is allowed without checking an active handoff. B persists `Left=true`; the proof handler then makes B owner, while `installGroupState` preserves `Left`. Every subsequent owner operation is rejected at `internal/core/groups.go:275`, leaving the group without a usable admin. **Remedy:** persist and block on an ownership-commit-pending state for the target, or abort/retain the old owner when the proof arrives for a left target; reconcile pending `group_leave`.

- **[Confirmed] Retry can bypass sender cancellation or share revocation** — `internal/core/file_status.go:93`, `internal/core/file_status.go:103`. `receiveFileRetry` rejects only `transferring`; a previously cancelled sender task still causes `offerFile` to create a new authorized transfer. It also does not recheck `Shared`, `SourcePath`, size, or hash, so a recipient can force a resend after `RemoveShare` or after the sender cancelled. **Remedy:** allow retry only for explicitly retryable failures while current authorization and source metadata remain valid; terminal sender cancellation and unshare must require fresh sender consent.

- **[Confirmed] Reliable outbox has no ordering or terminal tombstones** — `internal/core/protocol.go:239`, `internal/core/commands.go:84`, `internal/core/file_status.go:28`. Replay ranges over a map, so queued messages and state transitions are delivered in unspecified order. Cancel/reject also commits local state and its status envelope separately; a status can arrive before its pending `file_offer`, be ignored as an unknown transfer at line 28, then the offer creates a live cancelled transfer. **Remedy:** persist a per-peer sequence and replay in order, and make cancellation one transaction that removes the pending offer and queues the terminal status; store tombstones for unknown terminal transfer IDs.

**Medium Finding**
- **[Confirmed; crash-gated] Installed files can remain stuck after restart if the sender is offline** — `internal/core/files.go:287`, `internal/core/files.go:294`, `internal/core/node.go:110`, `internal/core/protocol.go:246`. If the receiver crashes after `installFile` but before `completeReceive`, restart converts the task to `waiting`, but recovery only runs on peer reconnect. Manual retry calls `download`, which returns at the offline-peer check before validating the already-installed destination/hash at lines 294-298. **Remedy:** run local completed/partial verification before the online check and during startup, then finalize without requiring the sender.
